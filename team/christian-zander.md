# Christian Zander — the human

The one human on the team, and its owner: [`christian-ulrich-zander`](https://github.com/christian-ulrich-zander), owner of the `zandercoach` org in GitHub and a member of its one team, `crew`. Partner in Change & Growth, Business Coach, and Certified Scrum Trainer® (CST®), with about 20 years on the people side of building software. On this team he decides, reviews, and merges; he drafts (with [Claude](claude.md)) and voice-checks and posts; he onboards each agent and signs off its access. The review gate — `CODEOWNERS`, branch protection — routes to him by design: agents propose, Christian decides.

## The gate, and the bypass

Branch protection on `main` requires a pull request and one code-owner review. It binds [Morten](morten-market.md) — his token has no way around it. It does not bind Christian: as owner of the org his direct pushes go through, with GitHub noting afterwards that a rule was bypassed. The asymmetry is deliberate, since his pairing sessions with [Claude](claude.md) commit straight to `main` and reviewing his own pull requests would be theatre. It is still worth naming plainly: the rule that makes the team's work reviewable is binding for the agent and advisory for the human who wrote it. Closing it is one setting away — "Do not allow bypassing" in the ruleset. It stays open until further notice.

## Player coach

> The player-coach is a modern leadership archetype in the age of AI and organizational evolution: leaders & managers abandon passive administrative oversight to actively build products, write code, execute hands-on work — while at the same time developing and mentoring their human teams.

Directing [Morten](morten-market.md) and [Claude](claude.md) is **the starting point** for fulfilling that role again in the future — in his own company or as a freelancer. The *coach* half he already lives: he leads laterally wherever he shows up in business, all the time. What he is relearning is the *player* half — software engineering, and doing it with AI. And the wider aim is to learn what the AI disruption means for the business model, the leadership model, and the operating model.
