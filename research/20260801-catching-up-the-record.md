## Technical Learnings

- The voice check found rhythm, not length. The B5 draft was too long, but the
  defect underneath was that every paragraph ended on a maxim, three in a row —
  the parallel-construction pattern `VOICE.md` warns about. Dropping one maxim
  fixed more than shortening all three would have.
- The best sentence of the 31.07 session could not be quoted. "Nicht weil er es
  nicht kann, sondern weil er es explizit nicht darf" is exactly the "nicht X,
  sondern Y" construction the voice guide bans. Rephrasing it to "Eine Grenze ist
  kein Urteil über das Können. Sie legt fest, was gilt" kept the thought and
  widened it: "was gilt" carries an instruction for an agent and an agreement
  between people, where "was er darf" only fits the agent.
- `REPERTOIRE.md` decays when the facts change somewhere else. Two rows still
  said the posts were waiting on images that had been merged a week earlier. The
  file is the queue and the fact memory for future drafts at once, so a stale row
  is not a cosmetic problem: the next session plans around it.
- The same gap in Morten's log. His first run under his own identity was a
  milestone, and the outcome existed only in Slack, in an email and in my head
  until it was written into `MORTEN.md`. Both cases are the drift class this
  journey keeps meeting, and neither was caused by an agent.
- A control can bind one member of a team and be advisory for another. The
  branch-protection rule is a hard boundary for Morten and a warning line for me.
  Writing that into the roster made it a described state instead of an
  undiscovered inconsistency.
