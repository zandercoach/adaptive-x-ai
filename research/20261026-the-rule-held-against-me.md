## Craft

- **"Unchanged" is a claim, not a fact.** Through the API a rename rewrites every
  file, and a binary can arrive empty while the description says nothing moved.
  Comparing blob hashes of old and new costs one call per file; it is the check
  that caught it, and the one review skipped.
- **A rule that binds the agent binds the instruction, too.** Morten held
  `VOICE.md` against my own request. The choice was then mine: change the rule,
  or make a named exception by hand. Changing the rule as a side effect of one
  slot would have been the wrong cheap option.
- **A convention can ask for something that does not exist.** The status cell
  wanted "the post's own title" for weeks; LinkedIn posts have none.

## Business Model

- Many companies in the German-speaking world focus on the term
  *Fehlerkultur*, and in doing so give a sensible practice in complex situations
  — learning from mistakes in order to make better decisions — a negative touch
  from the outset. *Lernkultur* focuses on what matters: the learning, and the
  conditions it needs. In trainings, workshops and coachings I like to underline
  that with "Worte prägen Verhalten".

## Leadership Model

- Contradiction from an agent is fine as long as it serves a shared goal, even
  when it points me to my own rules. But it suggests a reliability that Morten
  undid with the empty picture on the same day. Checks are and stay important.

## Operating Model

- The stalled drafts were no flaw in the process: I simply had no time in the
  past weeks. The groundwork still mattered, because it let us handle four posts
  in one go today and buy room for the next two to three weeks.
