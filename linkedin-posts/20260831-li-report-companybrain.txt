Adaptive × AI — The company gets a brain

More from the lab notebook of my learning journey on adaptive-x-ai: the instructions file at the root of this repository used to open with the sentence "This repo is Christian's second brain." That sentence is gone. It now says "our company brain", and rewriting it was the most useful thing in the session.

What forced it was an audit. My pairing agent carries knowledge of its own, in a memory that lives outside the repository, and I finally looked at what was in there. Six items, most of them already in the repo, so memory was holding duplicates that could quietly drift away from the originals. Exactly one was a genuine gap: the hosting setup, the DNS records, the domains, the legal note. It has a file now.

So instead of copying that memory in wholesale, I wrote down which layer holds what. The repository is authoritative. Memory is an index into it, plus the two things the repo should not hold: how the agent prefers to work with me, and whatever it is juggling for the next ten minutes. The new name carries that definition in the same sentence, because "company brain" on its own would have invited everything being dumped straight back in. A name can carry a discipline, or quietly lose one.

Then the roster. Who works here got its own place in the repo, separate from the config files the agents have, because those answer two different questions: the who and the how. Describing the two agents side by side was the part I had not expected. Morten is a member, with his own account and his name in the history. Claude is something else, a capability I wear: my Iron Man suit, powerful in my hands and inert without me in them. That asymmetry is the content of the roster, and not an awkwardness to smooth over.

My own entry I was interviewed for, one question at a time, and it corrected a framing I had let stand. I am not relearning how to lead people, I do that wherever I show up. What I am relearning is engineering, and doing it with AI. Player-coach, back in the game 😉

Full disclosure, as always: this is a learning report, not a how-to — what I set up, what nearly broke, and what I got wrong. Text and image were made together with AI, and I reviewed both before they went out. The work can be shared. Being accountable for it cannot.
